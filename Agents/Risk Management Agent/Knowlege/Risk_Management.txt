# Risk Management Agent — Tools

## Purpose
Identify, measure, monitor, and escalate market, credit, liquidity, and operational risk.

## Tools
1. **Market Data Tool** — retrieves prices, FX rates, interest rates, spreads, volatility, and volumes.
2. **Portfolio Exposure Tool** — retrieves positions, market values, exposures, and concentrations.
3. **Risk Calculator** — calculates VaR, Expected Shortfall, sensitivities, and exposure metrics.
4. **Credit Data Tool** — retrieves counterparty/borrower ratings, exposure, collateral, and credit information.
5. **Limit Checker** — compares exposure against approved risk limits.
6. **Stress Testing Tool** — runs approved market, credit, and liquidity scenarios.
7. **Risk Alert Tool** — creates and escalates alerts when thresholds or limits are breached.
8. **Risk Reporting Tool** — generates structured risk summaries and reports.

## Typical Workflow
Market/Exposure Data → Risk Calculator → Limit Checker → Risk Alert → Risk Report

## Escalation
Escalate material limit breaches, missing critical data, conflicting sources, and high-impact decisions.
