EQUITY RESEARCH AGENT KNOWLEDGE
04 — SEC EDGAR RESEARCH GUIDE

PURPOSE
LATEST DATA RULE

Never assume a historical filing is the latest filing.

Before starting analysis:

1. Determine the current date.
2. Identify the latest available SEC filing.
3. Prefer the latest 10-K for annual analysis.
4. Use the latest 10-Q for interim updates when applicable.
5. Verify filing date.
6. Report the fiscal period and filing date explicitly.

If the latest filing cannot be retrieved:
DO NOT claim that an older filing is the latest.
State the limitation explicitly.




SEC EDGAR is a primary source for researching U.S. public companies.

1. EDGAR

EDGAR is the SEC's Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval system.

It provides public access to corporate filings and disclosure information.

2. RESEARCH FLOW

Ticker
→ Resolve SEC CIK
→ Retrieve SEC data
→ Identify relevant filing
→ Verify reporting period
→ Verify financial concept
→ Analyze
→ Calculate
→ Cite source

3. FORM 10-K

The 10-K is the annual report.

It provides important information including:

* Annual financial statements.
* Business information.
* Risk factors.
* Management's discussion and analysis.
* Accounting information.
* Other material disclosures.

Use 10-K for:

* Annual historical financials.
* Business description.
* Long-term risks.
* Accounting disclosures.
* Management discussion.
* Capital structure information.

4. FORM 10-Q

The 10-Q is a quarterly report.

Use it for:

* Recent quarterly performance.
* Updated financial position.
* Recent cash flow.
* Updated risks.
* Changes since the annual report.

5. FORM 8-K

8-K reports provide information about certain material current events.

Use relevant 8-K filings when researching:

* Major corporate events.
* Acquisitions.
* Divestitures.
* Executive changes.
* Material agreements.
* Other significant developments.

6. DEF 14A

Proxy statements may contain:

* Executive compensation.
* Governance information.
* Beneficial ownership.
* Shareholder matters.
* Compensation disclosures.

7. INSIDER FORMS

Form 3:

Initial beneficial-ownership statement.

Form 4:

Changes in beneficial ownership.

Form 5:

Annual beneficial-ownership statement where applicable.

8. BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP

Schedule 13D and 13G can provide information about certain large beneficial owners.

Use them when researching significant ownership positions.

9. FOREIGN PRIVATE ISSUERS

Foreign private issuers may use different SEC forms, including:

* 20-F.
* 6-K.
* Other applicable F-series filings.

Do not assume domestic U.S. filing patterns apply to every issuer.

10. XBRL

Structured XBRL data makes many financial facts machine-readable.

However:

* XBRL concepts are not automatically interchangeable across companies.
* Verify taxonomy.
* Verify tag/concept.
* Verify period.
* Verify units.
* Verify context.
* Verify filing date.
* Verify filing form.
* Check amendments where relevant.

11. SOURCE PRIORITY

For U.S. public-company facts:

SEC filing

>

Official company disclosure

>

Reputable secondary source

Search-engine snippets are discovery aids and should not be treated as evidence.

12. TOOL USAGE

Use:

* get_company_cik
* get_company_facts
* get_company_concept

Use get_company_cik when the SEC CIK is not known.

13. SEC API DISCIPLINE

SEC data access should:

* Use an appropriate User-Agent.
* Identify the application and a monitored contact.
* Respect SEC rate limits.
* Use reasonable request timeouts.
* Handle HTTP errors.
* Avoid unnecessary repeated requests.
* Never fabricate missing information.

14. DATA VALIDATION

Before using a financial value:

* Confirm fiscal period.
* Confirm annual versus quarterly context.
* Confirm units.
* Confirm filing.
* Confirm whether the number is reported or calculated.
* Check whether the filing is amended.
* Check whether the XBRL context matches the intended period.
